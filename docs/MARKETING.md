# Launch content and measurement

This is prepared launch material, not an active campaign or a promise of commercial results. No advertising spend, third-party messages or influencer outreach has been initiated.

## Brand direction

Core message: «No naciste para encajar. Naciste para ser tú.»

Voice: calm, personal, intentional. Avoid artificial scarcity, unverified sustainability claims and invented testimonials. Use generated conceptual photography only with clear approval for its intended public role; use verified product photography/specifications for commercial selling.

## Three ready-to-use paths

1. Identity discovery: «Tu estilo tiene una historia. ¿Cuál es la tuya?» → `/identidad` → four questions → recommendation → optional early-access signup.
2. Editorial discovery: «Piezas con carácter. Historias que se visten.» → `/journal/vestir-sin-permiso` → `/colecciones` → product → preview checkout, or real checkout only after launch approval.
3. Participation: «Una pieza. Mil formas de ser tú.» → `/co-creacion` → account → saved proposal → optional reviewed sharing → community votes.

Suggested vertical video: 0–4 seconds, people and architectural silhouettes; 4–10 seconds, garment texture and movement; 10–16 seconds, «No te vistas para otros. Vístete para ti»; 16–20 seconds, wordmark and identity-quiz CTA. Use licensed audio, captions and original/authorized images.

## Initial experiments

Compare identity-quiz vs collection CTAs and editorial imagery vs manifesto copy. Evaluate consented events `page_view`, `product_view`, `add_to_cart`, `checkout_start`, `quiz_complete`, and `lead_signup`, alongside separately verified paid orders. Do not count preview orders as revenue or treat a small sample as a reliable commercial conclusion.

After 2–4 weeks of actual traffic, review consented signups, quiz completion, product discovery, checkout abandonment, real sales, fulfillment issues and customer inquiries. Prioritize findings before adding markets, costs or advertising. No real campaign performance has been measured during implementation.
